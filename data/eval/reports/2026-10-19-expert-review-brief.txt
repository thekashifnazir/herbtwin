Expert check of plant-care advice from an app (5 photos)

I'm building a plant-care app for UK home gardeners, mostly culinary herbs in pots and gardens, for a hackathon. For each of the 5 plant photos attached (photo-s012.jpg etc.), the app gave the advice below. I need a horticulturist or experienced professional gardener (UK experience ideal) to grade it. The expert's own judgement is the point: please don't grade with AI.

For each photo, please give:
1. What you see: the main problem, or "healthy", in one line.
2. The app's first action (the headline): Right / Acceptable / Wrong / Harmful, with one line why.
3. Anything wrong, misleading or confusing elsewhere in the advice (quote the line).
4. What you would tell the owner to do first, in one or two sentences.
5. Overall grade, 1 (useless or harmful) to 5 (what I'd tell them myself).

Deliverable: a spreadsheet or CSV, one row per photo (photo id, what you see, first-action grade, issues, your first step, overall grade), plus a short paragraph of general comments on the advice style.

Context: assume a UK garden in mid-October; the owner gave no other information. The advice text is exactly what the app shows (the app's page shows the same text in cards). The photos are public Creative Commons photos (credits below), not mine. I'd like to quote the overall results (grades and short quotes) in a public hackathon write-up, so please confirm that's fine.
