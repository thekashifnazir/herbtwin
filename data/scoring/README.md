# Side-by-side photo check

- `pairs.json`: pairs of photos of the same plant, used to check that a side-by-side
  comparison says the right direction of change (better, worse or the same), including
  parsley that recovered.
- `gate.json`: the result of that check (`scripts/check_scoring.py`).

The pair list and result are ours (MIT). **The photos are not included.** The public ones
keep their own licence (CC BY or CC BY-SA, from Wikimedia Commons, ETH Zurich and
PhenoCam), credited in the `note` field of `pairs.json`; the rest are the owner's own
garden photos.
