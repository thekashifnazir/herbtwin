{# the shared scroll reveal (23 Oct, Kash: "make the pages feel dynamic … things appear and are revealed in a way that makes sense"). A block opts in with data-reveal: rise, stagger (each child rises as it arrives), draw or grow (the page's sheet draws its lines or bars). herbtwin.css and the page's own sheet say how each moves, and only under html.reveal-on. What is on the first screen, or above it, is marked in (.is-in) before anything is held, so nothing in view ever waits; the rest is marked as it arrives (.is-arriving plays its motion), once, and blocks arriving together go 80 ms apart, top to bottom, left to right. With no script, no observer or Reduce Motion, reveal-on is never set and nothing is ever held. It starts after the first-load intro, if one plays (intro-playing, then intro-done and the herbtwin:intro-done event), and after 2 s at most. It replaces home's own reveal (H2) #}
