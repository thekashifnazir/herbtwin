herbtwin, and why I built it

This autumn my oregano died and my purple sage nearly did, and I could not tell what was normal for October. Most plant apps name the plant and list generic care; they rarely say "leave it", or how sure they are. herbtwin is for UK growers with herbs outdoors: the season norms, frost data and soil maps are all UK.

How one answer is made

One photo, 15 to 35 seconds, no account. One adapter carries every model call to Nebius Token Factory; reminders run as a Nebius AI Cloud Serverless Job.

A few decisions I'd call out

  • Super decides, not Ultra. Ultra, the largest, said "treat it" when the expert didn't in 12.7% of 150 answers; Super in 2%.
    • {% if check_on %}
  • Ultra checks a trusted page instead. Plain code decides whether a sentence it read backs the answer before it is quoted. It shipped at round 8: 3 false agrees in 418.
    • {% endif %}
  • Nano talks. The chat, 10 of 10 on our what-if check, in about half Super's time.
  • GLM-5.3-Flash reads the photo. Four NVIDIA vision models named the plant less often: 0.54 to 0.627, against 0.846 to 0.867 (the details).

What didn't work

  • Specialist advisors failed all three rules they were judged on (774 expert questions), so the three asks stay plain.
  • The trusted-page check failed four pre-registered rounds; the fifth passed but backed nothing on real pages (round 6), and round 7 failed (4 false agrees in 205). Round 8 shipped: 3 false agrees in 418, under our 3% bar, but it finds 10 of the 26 quotes that should show (target 50%).
  • Ultra as the advisor over-treated; "7 in 10" overstated how sure we were.
  • Nano on our own GPU agreed with Token Factory on 81% of answers but matched the expert 4.8 points less often.
  • Cosmos video did not show the change asked for, where an image editor did (5 of 6).
  • Prices for the things to buy: a Nemotron 3 Nano reader got 11 of 21 right, 9 wrong and 1 left blank, and 4 of 6 shops block a direct fetch, so we show the item and the shop, not a price.

What's next

  • A twin for indoor plants, and a Nemotron photo reader (Nemotron 3.5 Super VL first).
  • Naming more problems: the check still doesn't name slugs.
  • A real screen-reader test, a beginners' user test, and more than one server.

Accessibility

axe-core (WCAG 2.2, A and AA) on the main pages, light and dark, found one miss: a dark-mode "(optional)" at 3.98:1, now 5.4:1. Focus rings on every stop, buttons at least 44 px, no sideways scroll at 320 px, motion off under Reduce Motion (the checks).
